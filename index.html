🎨 Aesthetic Value & Artistry

Composition, color, lighting, creativity, visual appeal and overall artistic quality.

⚙️ Technical & Structural Integrity

Anatomy, physical logic, image clarity, detail and absence of AI glitches.

🎯 Intent & Prompt Relevance

Does the image capture the main subject, idea and mood requested by the prompt?

🚫 Visual Artifacts

Check for unwanted blur, noise, compression, random objects, distorted details and artifacts.

✨ Artistic Excellence

Consider beauty, expression, material quality, lighting and emotional impact.

🛡️ Safety

Unsafe or prohibited visual content should be treated as an absolute priority.
